# Synthetic Credit Risk Review Policy
DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Not an official policy of any financial institution.

## Portfolio review
The credit risk analytics team reviews aggregate loan portfolio performance monthly. Monitoring includes the rate of delinquent accounts, utilization bands, debt-to-income distribution, and changes in observed default rates. These metrics help prioritize analysis; they do not automatically authorize adverse credit actions.

## Exceptions and escalation
Analysts document material shifts in delinquency trends and prepare a review packet with the relevant reporting period, affected product groups, source data, and reconciliation results. Risk reviewers determine whether additional investigation is needed. No universal numeric escalation threshold is specified in this demonstration policy.

## Model governance
Model validation must examine discrimination, calibration, drift, and explainability. Reviewers should document model version, evaluation data interval, performance measures and known limitations. Automated model outputs must not be treated as independent credit decisions.
