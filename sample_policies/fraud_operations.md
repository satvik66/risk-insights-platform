# Synthetic Fraud Investigation Procedure
DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Not an official banking procedure.

## Monitoring signals
Investigators may review unusual transaction amounts, rapid sequences of activity, changes in device fingerprint, and geographic anomalies. A model risk score is only a prioritization signal and is not proof of fraud.

## Analyst workflow
Alerts are ranked for analyst review. The investigator checks transaction context and available historical account activity and records the reason for the disposition. Cases with unresolved information are escalated for additional review. Automated systems must not independently freeze customer accounts in this demo.

## Audit trail
Every review should record an event identifier, analyst decision, timestamp, the evidence considered, and any follow-up action. Only authorized personnel should access sensitive case data.
